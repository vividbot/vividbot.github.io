Project Overview

"the walls are damp and chipped, flickering lights barley guide your path. You shouldn't be here. Blood of unknown malicious origin splatters the wall, the stench if decay fills your lungs. Something evil is lurking just behind the walls, you can just feel it."

This piece was created with the intention of furthering my ability to create PBR ready tileable materials for unreal. These materials feature vertex painting through layers and three different variations for each material made.

View on ArtStation

Project Details

  • Role: Environment Artist
  • Tools: Unreal Engine 5, Substance Painter, Blender
  • Focus: Lighting, modularity, materials
  • Year: 2024

Approach

Built a modular environment with careful attention to scale, silhouette, and material variation to create a believable and cinematic scene.

Key Notes

Focused on performance-friendly lighting, PBR materials, and a clear visual hierarchy to guide the viewer through the scene.
